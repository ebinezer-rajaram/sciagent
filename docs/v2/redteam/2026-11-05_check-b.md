## Blocking

**1. The S16 inference overreaches** (`deviate-run2.txt`, `controls.py:1222,1242`). The premise holds for values: the bootstrap reads crn r 128-255 only. "Never read" is too strong, because P1 and `e_cal` read those runs' outcomes (`controls.py:1096-1116`), immaterial here since all are complete. The conclusion fails:
- Independence: under one law the halves are independent, so S16 says nothing about how far selection lifted H16. It bears only on the law.
- One noisy pool: S16 is 4 of 400 pools (z +1.71), post hoc, and overlapping pools understate spread. Eight disjoint law blocks give p16(MG) `.629 .876 .753 .893 .693 .852 .818 .754` (sd 0.093), so agreement between S16 and H16 is weak evidence of a common cause.
- Small effect on the law: adding S16 moves p16(MG) from 0.7942 to 0.8125, about +2 E points of a 19.4-point gap. "Selection explains about 9 of the 19.7" is not overturned.
- p16 only: every cal run feeding p32 was read, so no unselected E exists. `H32 x S16` (109.99) must not be cited as unselected, and `S32 x S16` (106.11) contains a read half too.
- Chance and a low law estimate remain open. The check (a) CI upper end is 0.86.
