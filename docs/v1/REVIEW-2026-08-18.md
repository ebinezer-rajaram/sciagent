## 1. Headline

The engineering is unusually strong — the determinism machinery, the sealed
engine, the gate discipline, and the measured-not-asserted culture would be
credible in front of any reviewer. The single most important adverse fact is
this: **the recorded 56-cell matrix cannot yet answer the headline question,
for three independent measurement-layer reasons**, none of which is about the
agent:

1. **D4 is identically zero by construction** across all 1,120 ledger rows
   (`eval/matrix.py:543` passes the full entertained set — which always
   contains the candidate — so "mine − best" is never positive). Verified
   read-only against `spec9.db`: `d4 = 0` on 1120/1120 rows. The DECISIONS
   claim "D4 bit-identical between V7 and V1" reports a constant as a
   comparison.
2. **The preregistered contrast conditions on a variable that never fires for
   V7.** `CellReading.inadequate` records the whole-record PPC (0/20 on
   V7/S11) while V7 acted on the Stage A gate (17/20). `report_matrix
   --contrast` exits 3 by design. This is the §12-criterion-4 incoherence
   `docs/OPEN-DECISIONS.md` §1 already writes up; the matrix confirms it at
   scale.
3. **V7's D3 on S11 is bit-identical to V1's on all 20 replicates** (verified
   from the ledger). The recorded V7-vs-B4/B5 non-overlap on D3 therefore
   measures BOED-with-a-library against rotation-with-a-library — selection
   policy and library, not proposal extension. The comparator the criterion
   names (a B6-equivalent random structured generator) does not exist anywhere
   in the tree.

None of this is hidden — DECISIONS.md and OPEN-DECISIONS.md state most of it
candidly, which is itself to the repository's credit. But it means the honest
current status is: **apparatus complete and verified; the science it exists to
produce is not yet measurable from what was recorded.** The ranked plan in §5
is ordered around fixing exactly that, and §6 designs the experiments that
become possible once it is fixed. The transcripts corpus
(`.cache/transcripts/spec9.json`) makes most of the re-measurement free:
recorded LLM calls replay deterministically, so fixing the scoring layer and
re-deriving costs compute, not quota.

## 2. What is already strong

Credit where it is due, with evidence — each item re-verified by the
refutation pass, several by independent execution.

- **Determinism is engineered, not hoped for.** Name-derived streams via
  blake2b `SeedSequence` spawn keys (`core/program.py:74-96`), sorted tie-break
  topological order, repr-sorted `FrozenDict`, frozen `EventLog` arrays; A1's
  subprocess arm runs three `PYTHONHASHSEED` values including `random`
  (`test_a01_a05.py:91-116`), which genuinely discriminates hash-derived
  streams. `.gitattributes` ties line-ending policy to the invariant with
  auditable reasons. The README quickstart reproduced **byte-identically** when
  a reviewer ran it cold this session.
- **The sealed engine is defended adversarially, with working exploits.**
  `test_systems.py` ships a Liar, Poisoner, Smuggler, Forger and LateActor,
  each demonstrating its attack and asserting the typed refusal; `EngineView`
  withholds the mutating surface; `campaign._reconcile` compares quantities of
  different provenance and **states its own scope honestly**
  (`campaign.py:517-519`) instead of implying completeness.
- **Measurement culture.** The PPC combiner was chosen by a five-way measured
  comparison with the losers' failure numbers recorded (Fisher size 0.130,
  ACAT power 0.000); the mechanism calibration reproduced at an unused seed
  (Seed 777, 8000 events) during this review; A24's 10% bar is proven to
  discriminate (random selection scores 55.52% and must fail; the table policy
  scores 99.33% — reproduced by execution in 1.9s).
- **Anti-vacuity discipline in the gates.** Nearly every all-rejected gate has
  a positive control and a case-count assertion; A14 plants both a violation
  and a clean fixture; A17's licence boundary is tested in both directions and
  a forged value is refused at runtime.
- **Scientific honesty against incentive.** The Hawkes D3 figure was revised
  0.960 → 0.697 when a battery bug was fixed, although dropping the fix would
  have preserved the published number. A drafted §12-criterion-4 rewrite was
  withdrawn unshipped because it was written in the same session as the
  measurement — invariant 6 applied to the authors themselves.
  `docs/OPEN-DECISIONS.md` exists precisely to take those decisions cold.
- **Record/replay is genuinely sound** for what it covers: content-addressed
  transcripts, replay that raises on any miss, an append-only store that
  refuses divergent payloads, and a test that replays a recorded run
  byte-identically against a provider that raises if consulted.
- **Registry legibility.** Three enforcement layers with the
  REPLACE/recursive_triggers hazard documented in the module docstring;
  content-address injectivity defended by length-prefixed encoding and tested
  against delimiter collision and `PYTHONHASHSEED`; the prepared-statement
  cache bypass of sqlite's authorizer was found, fixed and fuzzed.
- **Docstrings state guarantees and rejected alternatives**, not summaries of
  code. The condition algebra names its domain restriction and failure
  direction; the ledger states its own limitation and where the job lands
  instead; performance exceptions carry measured nanosecond figures.

### E4 — Real-data grounding: the SCEDC QTM catalog

The corpus evaluation ran as a pipeline (datasheet from the actual
`pointproc` code → three family surveys with fetched documentation → judge →
adversarial verification against SCEDC's live servers). **Chosen: the QTM
catalog** (Ross et al. 2019, Science): 1.81M template-matched Southern
California events 2008–2017, two text files (192MB + 95MB) verified
byte-frozen since 2019-04-15 — natively the citable snapshot the append-only
registry needs. **Runner-up:** Binance spot aggTrades monthly zips (exact
(time, size, sign) schema, checksummed immutable files; loses on licence
ambiguity and on superposed mechanisms with no single-edit consensus truth).

**Why QTM.** The century-deep consensus mechanism — ETAS, magnitude-gated
triggering — **is exactly one library edit**: `AddDependency(size→arrival)`,
verbatim `mechanisms.py:146`, i.e. S11's out-of-library mechanism, whose sole
detector is `size_gap_correlation`. ETAS has been fit to *this catalog
specifically* (Moutote 2021; van den Ende & Ampuero 2020). The transfer test
is therefore: does the system, holding the closed set, detect inadequacy on
data where a century of seismology says the truth is the S11 edit — and does
Stage B propose it?

**Mapping.** arrival ← origin time rescaled to mean gap 1.0; size ← M − Mc
(Gutenberg–Richter ⇒ approximately exponential — the reference's own size
family); sign ← constant (negative-control diagnostic degrades to 0.0 as
designed); seeds ← preregistered disjoint 512-event segments; DATA_VERSION ←
snapshot SHA256; declared S12-style nuisance ← short-term aftershock
incompleteness + the 9.5σ threshold; found D3 battery ← in-network M5+ events
(Chino Hills 2008, Borrego Springs 2016 — El Mayor-Cucapah 2010 sits at the
network edge, a verified caveat).

**Two preregistered tracks** (do not mix): *semi-synthetic* — recalibrate the
reference to QTM's operating point, author edits, simulate: full D1–D6
survives; *found-data* — the ETAS consensus edit is the D1 surrogate; D2, D4,
D6 survive intact, D5 restricted to observational designs, D3 only as the
found battery, reported as such. R4 is answerable without interventions as
**rank preservation under D2**.

**Design.** (1) Snapshot, hash, register. (2) Ingestion pipeline as versioned
environment code (segmenting, magnitude cut, tie rule, rescale) with its own
A-gate (determinism: snapshot hash → byte-identical EventLog segments). (3)
Stage A on found segments: does the probe fire on QTM segments where it stays
quiet on the calibrated null? 20 segments × the conventional arms first;
size on semi-synthetic null segments. (4) Stage B: V7 on the same segments —
does it propose the size→arrival edit? (5) Semi-synthetic arm to bound the
detection-artifact risk the verifier found: template matching in large-event
codas produces false detections clustered after large marks — the same
signature as the consensus edit — so the S11-detector's reading must be
bounded on simulated ETAS with and without the artifact model.

**Known risks (adversarially verified).** Licence: free research use with
three citations, but no formal licence text — get written SCEDC confirmation
before redistribution of the snapshot. Regional pooling mixes Mc and
background rate (preregister a polygon sensitivity arm). Timestamp quantum
0.01s vs mean gap ~174s+: four orders of margin.

**Runtime/cost.** Pipeline + calibration ≈ the XL row in §5. Compute:
segment prep minutes; semi-synthetic table rebuilds ≈ the existing ~3 min per
structure set; conventional arms $0. V7 on 20 found segments ≈ 40 calls ≈
**$4–8** live (at the stale $0.098 figure; re-baseline first — see E6).
