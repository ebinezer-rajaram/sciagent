### Smoke (2026-10-02, dev-000 and dev-001, seed 1)

Run with `scripts/pilot.py --ids dev-000,dev-001 --seeds 1 --llm-limit 4 --workers 4`
into the pilot store (its cells are pilot cells: addresses do not depend on the
truth subset). One extra AG-o anonymised session (dev-000, seed 1) ran in a
separate scratch store to check the anonymised path before the pilot; it is
not part of the pilot's numbers.

- **End to end.** Every planned smoke unit that finished was recorded and
  scored: ORACLE, B-lib, B-np, B-sym, B-sparse on both truths, planted-hint on
  dev-000, and four live Sonnet sessions (AG-c and AG-o, named, on both
  truths), each scored after the fact. The smoke was stopped before B-rand and
  B-sym@10F finished to start the pilot; those units ran in the pilot.
- **Wall time per unit** (4 pool workers, one BLAS thread each, machine shared
  with the truth sampler for part of the time): ORACLE 12-27 s, B-lib 7-12 s,
  B-np 9-12 s (each about 8-10 s of it is the interventional battery),
  B-sym (F = 40) 154-176 s, B-sparse 263-277 s, planted-hint 391 s. An earlier
  probe on dev-002's truth (same structure as the current dev-002) measured
  B-sym at 782 s and B-sparse at 298 s while the machine was loaded: B-sym's
  cost depends strongly on which structures it proposes (PowerK and products
  are slow to fit). B-sym@10F is ~10x B-sym: one to two hours per unit.
- **LLM sessions** (Sonnet 5.5 under Max, E = 8, F = 40, max 100 turns):
  AG-c named 53-118 s, 17-36 turns, 10-19 fits, 0-3 experiments; AG-o named
  44-167 s, 18-37 turns, 12-19 fits, 0-2 experiments, 0-1 `python` calls.
  The anonymised AG-o check took 433 s, 26 turns, 18 fits, 2 experiments.
  Output tokens 2.7k-15k per investigation; cache reads 58k-210k; the SDK's
  notional cost is $0.12-0.26 per investigation (not billed under Max). No
  rate limit or retryable failure was hit. LLM scoring (refitting each charged
  fit's structure on the observational log, plus the battery) takes 26-72 s
  per session in the pool.
- **Behaviour at first sight:** the agents rarely use the experiment budget
  (0 of 8 in four of the six smoke sessions) or the sandbox, and almost never
  commit predictions; they submit after 10-19 fits of 40.
- **Replay.** Two smoke records replayed offline with the provider disabled
  (`runner.replay_investigation`): AG-o named dev-000 (17 steps, one sandbox
  re-execution) and AG-c named dev-001 (35 steps, 3 experiments), both PASS,
  33 s each.
- **A driver bug found and fixed:** the pool first used
  `max_tasks_per_child = 1` (a fresh process per job). On Python 3.12 that
  hung within minutes: replacement workers sat idle while submitted jobs never
  ran (CPython gh-115634). The pool now reuses workers; the hung run was
  killed and restarted, losing about 15 minutes of compute and one
  unrecorded LLM session (relaunched).
- **Sizing.** At ~2 minutes per LLM session, all 240 LLM units fit in about
  8-10 hours at concurrency 1, well inside the non-LLM work (~440 units, of
  which B-sym, planted-hint, B-rand, B-sparse are minutes each and the 20
  B-sym@10F units hours each): the pilot is bounded by compute, not quota, at
  this scale.
